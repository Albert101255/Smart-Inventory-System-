HOW TRANSACTIONS AFFECT FORECASTS

Every STOCK OUT transaction both reduces physical stock and increases the product's 30-day outgoing consumption total (consumed_30d).

Prediction Engine Formula (app/prediction_engine.py):
1. avg_daily = total_out_last_30_days / 30
2. days_remaining = current_quantity / avg_daily
3. if days_remaining < 7: trigger LOW_STOCK Alert

Try recording a STOCK OUT of 45 units on Laptop Pro x1 or Wireless Mouse and watch it immediately drop below 7 days and trigger an alert!
